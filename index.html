Privacy Policy

Magic Diamond Painting Art Studio

Effective date: April 15, 2026

✨ Summary: This app processes images locally on your device. It may display ads to non-premium users and offers a one-time premium purchase through Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

Magic Diamond Painting Art Studio does not require you to create an account or provide personal details in order to use the app.

The app may store limited local preferences on your device, such as language settings, free usage counters, or premium status, so the app can function correctly.

2. How the App Works

All image processing in this app is performed locally on your device. Images you load into the app are not uploaded by us to our own servers. Your images remain under your control on your device.

3. Permissions

The app may request access to your device's photo gallery, media, or storage solely to allow you to select, process, and save images. This access is used only for the app’s functionality.

4. Advertising

The app may show advertisements to users who are not using the premium version. These ads may be provided by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect certain device identifiers, approximate location, usage data, or other technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies in order to deliver and improve ads.

5. In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time premium purchase to unlock premium features. Payments are processed securely by Google Play.

We do not collect or store your payment card or billing information. Payment information is handled by Google Play according to its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play Billing. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. However, third-party services such as advertising providers may process technical data as described in their own policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app safe and to minimize data handling. Most image processing stays on your device, and we do not operate our own image-storage servers for the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

1. Información que recopilamos

Magic Diamond Painting Art Studio no requiere que crees una cuenta ni que proporciones datos personales para utilizar la aplicación.

La app puede guardar en tu dispositivo algunas preferencias limitadas, como el idioma, el contador de uso gratuito o el estado premium, para que funcione correctamente.

Todo el procesamiento de imágenes se realiza localmente en tu dispositivo. Las imágenes que cargues en la app no se suben a nuestros propios servidores. Tus imágenes permanecen bajo tu control en tu dispositivo.

La app puede solicitar acceso a tu galería, archivos multimedia o almacenamiento únicamente para permitirte seleccionar, procesar y guardar imágenes. Este acceso se utiliza solo para el funcionamiento de la aplicación.

Los proveedores de publicidad pueden recopilar determinados identificadores del dispositivo, ubicación aproximada, datos de uso u otra información técnica de acuerdo con sus propias políticas de privacidad para mostrar y mejorar los anuncios.

La app ofrece una compra única para desbloquear funciones premium. Los pagos se procesan de forma segura a través de Google Play.

La app puede utilizar servicios de terceros como Google AdMob y Google Play Billing. Estos servicios pueden procesar datos conforme a sus propias políticas de privacidad y obligaciones legales.

Tomamos medidas razonables para mantener la app segura y minimizar el tratamiento de datos. La mayor parte del procesamiento de imágenes permanece en tu dispositivo y no utilizamos servidores propios para almacenar tus imágenes.
