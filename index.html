2. Our Privacy Principles

  • No accounts required. None of our apps require you to register or log in.

  • No selling. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.
  • Offline-capable. Most features work without an internet connection. Internet is only used for ads (where applicable) and in-app purchases.

3. What Data We Collect

3.1 Data you enter into the app

Depending on the specific app, you may voluntarily enter data such as:

  • A display name or nickname
  • App-specific content you create: notes, tasks, calendar events, mood entries, shift schedules, diamond painting patterns, stock holdings, accounting entries, payroll/tax calculations, vocabulary progress, etc.
  • Preferences (theme, language, settings)
  • Images or photos you load into the app (only for apps with image-editing features such as Magic Diamond Painting)

This data is stored only on your device (using Android local storage, AsyncStorage, SQLite, or localStorage) and never transmitted to our servers. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data from Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

3.2 Data collected automatically by third-party services

For apps that display ads or process payments, certain third-party services automatically collect data as described in sections 5 and 6.

3.3 Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect: your email address, phone number, real name, physical address, contacts, SMS, call logs, precise location, health records, banking credentials, or biometric data. We do not use analytics SDKs that track personal usage (such as Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, Crashlytics, etc.) unless explicitly stated for a specific app.

4. Permissions Explained

Individual apps may request some or all of the following Android permissions. Each permission is only used for its stated purpose within that specific app:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — required for apps that show ads or process purchases, and to verify network availability.
  • VIBRATE — subtle haptic feedback on button taps.
  • BILLING / com.android.vending.BILLING — used by apps that offer premium in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • AD_ID / com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — used by apps that show ads, to read the resettable Android Advertising ID.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — only in apps that need to load images you select (e.g. Magic Diamond Painting). We only access images you explicitly pick through the system picker; we do not scan your gallery.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — only when an app needs to save a file (PDF, image, export) to your device's storage.
  • CAMERA — only in apps that offer camera capture. We do not record in the background.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT — only in apps offering optional biometric lock (e.g. Agenda Lady diary lock). Authentication happens locally; we never see your biometric data.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — only in apps that offer local reminders or alarms (e.g. Agenda Lady tasks, Turnos Lady shifts). Notifications are scheduled locally; we do not send push notifications from a server.
💡 Some apps may not require any network permissions at all and work 100% offline.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some of our apps show advertisements to non-premium users, served exclusively by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Currently we use interstitial ads (full-screen between actions) and do not display banner ads.

To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Android Advertising ID (a unique, resettable identifier)
  • IP address and approximate (city-level) location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, time viewed)
  • Non-personal crash and diagnostic data

This data is used for ad delivery, ad measurement, frequency capping, analytics and fraud prevention.

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or opt out of personalized ads entirely.

For full details, see:

  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps
  • Google AdMob Policies
  • Google Privacy Policy

5.1 Personalized vs. non-personalized ads (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before personalized ads are served, through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can withdraw consent at any time from the app (where applicable) or by resetting your Advertising ID.

6. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Some of our apps offer optional one-time premium purchases (e.g. to remove ads or unlock unlimited features). We do not offer recurring subscriptions.

All payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see, collect or store your:

  • Payment card number, expiration or CVV
  • Billing address
  • Google account email (beyond what Google tells us is needed to verify the purchase)

We only receive a confirmation token from Google Play that a purchase was successful, which we use to activate premium features locally on your device. For Google Play's privacy practices, see Google Payments Privacy Notice.

7. Third-Party Services

Depending on the specific app, the following third-party services may be used, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK — for advertising.
  • Google Play Billing — for in-app purchases.
  • Google Fonts — some apps load fonts from fonts.googleapis.com; Google may log your IP.
  • TradingView widgets — only in Acciones ViBo (financial charting widgets).

8. Children's Privacy

Our apps are intended for a general audience aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA where applicable). Our apps are not designed for or directed at children. If you believe a child has used one of our apps and provided personal information, contact us to request deletion.
