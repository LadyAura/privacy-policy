Privacy Policy

Magic Diamond Painting Art Studio

Effective date: April 11, 2026

✨ Summary: This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All image processing is done entirely on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Magic Diamond Painting Art Studio does not collect any personal information from its users. The app does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details.

2. How the App Works

All image processing in this app is performed locally on your device. Images you load into the app are never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party. Your images remain entirely private and under your control.

3. Permissions

The app may request access to your device's photo gallery or storage solely to allow you to load images for processing. This access is used only within the app and no image data is transmitted externally.

4. Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with any external parties.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your region). Since we collect no data at all, there is no risk to children's privacy.

6. Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data at risk. All processing stays on your device at all times.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
